# Lensy privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Lensy is published by Kettlebird. **Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.**

## What the app collects

Lensy itself collects nothing. It has no account, no advertising and no crash-reporting service of its own. We do not receive, store or sell any information about you or your documents.

## Where your data lives

Everything you scan, import, recognise or export is stored in the app's private storage on your device: page images, recognised text, titles, folders and receipt details. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling Lensy removes it. App backups are turned off, so the data is not copied to cloud backups either.

## No network permission

Lensy does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so the app itself cannot send data over the network. Text recognition runs on the device.

## Google Play services and Google Play

Some features are provided by Google software on your phone, not by Lensy:

- **Document scanner and text recognition language packs:** run through Google Play services (ML Kit). Play services downloads language packs on Lensy's behalf. Google's ML Kit may send Google diagnostics and usage statistics: device model and Android version, the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier, timings and error codes. Google states this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. It never includes your page images or recognised text. Lensy does not see or control this traffic; it is covered by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Lensy Pro (one-time purchase):** handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether the purchase is active. Lensy receives no payment details or personal information.

## Sharing

When you tap Share or Save, the file you chose goes to the app or location you pick. After that it is governed by that app's or service's policy. Lensy deletes its temporary export copies when it next starts, once they are more than 24 hours old.

## Children

Lensy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
